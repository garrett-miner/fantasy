Non-PPR Playbook

How standard changes the math

Volume backs win

Early-down carries and goal-line work are where the points live. A 20-carry back with no receiving role holds his value; in PPR he'd slide.

Slot receivers sink

High-catch, low-yardage receivers lose their floor. Favor receivers who win downfield or in the red zone.

Touchdowns swing weeks

With no reception cushion, scores make up a bigger share of every line. Expect more boom-and-bust — plan your bench for it.

Tight ends are thin

Outside the top few, most tight ends are touchdown-or-nothing. Lock in a reliable one or stream the matchups.
